About Crescentech

A Kenyan technology and creative studio built on one belief — there is future in technology.

Our story

Design and technology, done properly

Crescentech was started in 2019 by Barasa Briston — a graphic designer, web & systems developer and network technician based in Chuka, Kenya.

The idea was simple: businesses shouldn't need four different people to look professional and run smoothly. We design the brand, build the website, develop the system and install the network — so everything works together and you always know who to call.

Today we serve businesses, schools, churches, institutions and individuals with quality work at fair prices.
